About Fixipedia

A living archive for the stories behind aviation names.

Preserve the names

Fixipedia collects the origin stories behind waypoints, fixes, airports, and navaids so they are easier to find, preserve, and verify.

Add the context

The goal is to pair the official catalog with source-backed context from pilots, controllers, dispatchers, historians, and curious people.
